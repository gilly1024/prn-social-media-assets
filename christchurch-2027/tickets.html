Important information before booking or entering

Accessibility at Christchurch Mansion

Christchurch Mansion is a historic building with uneven flooring and steps in some areas. There is no lift to the first floor, so upstairs rooms may not be accessible to wheelchair users or visitors who cannot use stairs. An accessible toilet is available on the ground floor.

If you or your guest have mobility or other accessibility requirements, please contact PRN before booking or entering the free draw. We can discuss the intended investigation route, access limitations and reasonable adjustments that may be possible. Access to specific rooms and arrangements for this evening event are subject to venue approval.
