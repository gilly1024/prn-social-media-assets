# Controlled creative AI methods — PRN social and promotion (6 November 2026)

**Status: approved workflow method for suitable commissioned promotional work; no image/GIF integration, subscription, batch runner or auto-posting is claimed.** The existing [SOCIAL_CONTENT_ENGINE.md](SOCIAL_CONTENT_ENGINE.md), [BRAND_VOICE.md](BRAND_VOICE.md), [VIDEO_STYLE_GUIDE.md](VIDEO_STYLE_GUIDE.md), [VIDEO_EXPORT_AND_PLAYBACK_LOCK.md](VIDEO_EXPORT_AND_PLAYBACK_LOCK.md) and [PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md](PUBLISHING_WORKFLOW.md) remain the actual authorities. This note makes the owner's 6 November AI review actionable when a **currently assigned** creative job benefits from controllable image edits, faster drafting or animated GIFs.

## Decision and allowed uses

Owner is interested in image/GIF editing advances, especially consistency of **the actual official PRN logo** and clear promotional delivery. Use the **currently available and actually connected image tool** only where its capabilities are independently observable; announcements of model version '2.6', faster speed or lower cost are not proof of availability through an API or another agent. Do not purchase plans, generate automatically as a dependency, create recurring jobs or publish without specific permission.

For **PRN Halloween event posters, ticket promotions and original educational diagrams**, start with authentic approved venue photos and real logo source; make carefully controlled derivative layout, promotional illustration or motion only if source rights and disclosure are satisfied. State what is photographic evidence versus illustration.

For **documentary historic cases, case-archive/location images, investigations and evidence**, only authentic rights-cleared real location and historic images may be represented as actual. **Never** fabricate a historically accurate photo, investigator, witness, ghost, camera screenshot, equipment recording or purported paranormal event. A fictional artistic treatment must be clearly identified as such; don't replace approved actual venue imagery with a generated substitute.

For **logo**, use the authorised PRN blue P, metallic R, orange N and three rings. Do not substitute generic PR/N marks, invent letters or present a recreated approximation as the original. If the original asset isn't available, stop that visual edit and retain the genuine existing artwork.

## Job acceptance flow — mandatory when creative AI is involved

1. **Identify approved project task, platform and rights-cleared source** in the normal social content brief. Confirm original logo asset and the source photo. No fantasy imagery presented as historic.
2. **Match creative method:** direct crop/color/edit on actual photograph for factual context; image generation only for explicitly original artwork; GIF/motion only if it improves a real marketing deliverable.
3. **Generate/edit off to the side:** keep original source unchanged; retain version/credit, tool/version **as actually observed**, edit prompt, final dimensions, output path and authorisation.
4. **Critic QA:** logo exactness, visual source identity (does it really show Christchurch Mansion/Framlingham Castle/etc?), text legibility, no overlapping QR, safe contrast, no rapid-flash/seizure effects. For QR posters, generate **real QR from verified event URL** and scan it before printing; not via image guesswork.
5. **Media delivery QA:** real JPEG when the upload requires JPEG; MP4 per playback lock for video; animated GIF only when channel supports and a real loop actually plays. Do not claim a rendered still equals a valid video.
6. **Final approval and post:** do not automatically schedule or publish. After owner approval, use the canonical existing Postiz handoff; inspect destination readback, duplicate and real asset before reporting posted. Keep exact PRN CTA policy for Facebook/TikTok.
7. **Evidence record:** add selected method, actual tool confirmation, source photo rights, genuine logo hash/version, independent QA and owner release to the existing asset handoff. No new social memory vault or repeated maintenance job.

## Related non-creative methods

Execution integrity and source-linked provenance are already part of PRN's approved AI programme, tracked in [PRN OS #90](https://github.com/gilly1024/prn-operating-system/issues/90). Methods such as paid Kimi/DeepSeek, autonomous agent outbound calls and real-time video inference are **not** authorised by a marketing artwork request. Keep witness details and private field evidence outside model providers without separate rights and consent.

**Owner-facing outcome classification:** `METHOD DOCUMENTED` ≠ `ASSET GENERATED` ≠ `QA PASSED` ≠ `OWNER APPROVED` ≠ `PUBLISHED AND READ BACK`.
